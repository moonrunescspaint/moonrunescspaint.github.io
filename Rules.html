Rules

By using this website (the "site"), you agree that you'll follow these rules, and understand that if we reasonably think you haven't followed these rules, we may (at our own discretion) terminate your access to the site:


Global Rules

  1. You will not upload, post, discuss, request, or link to anything that violates local or United States law.
  2. You will not post any of the following:
    1. Troll posts
    2. Racism
    3. Links to Anthropomorphic ("furry") pornography
    4. Links to Grotesque ("guro") images
    5. Links to Loli/shota pornography
  3. You will not post or request personal information ("dox") or calls to invasion ("raids") towards other websites.
  4. The quality of posts is extremely important to this community. Contributors are encouraged to provide high-quality images and informative comments. Please refrain from posting the following:
    1. Irrelevant catchphrases or copypasta
      Example: "What the fuck did you just fucking say about me, you little bitch?..."
    2. Indecipherable text
      Example: "lol u tk him 2da bar|?"

    4. Ironic shitposting
      Example: "upboads for le funy maymay trollololololoxdxdxdxd~~!"
    5. Gibberish text
      Example: "jihgpndjnjbnb[oposfwf"
  5. Submitting false or misclassified reports, or otherwise abusing the reporting system may result in a ban.
  6. No spamming or flooding of any kind. No intentionally evading spam or post filters.
  7. Advertising (all forms) is not welcome—this includes any type of referral linking, "offers", soliciting, begging, stream threads, etc.
